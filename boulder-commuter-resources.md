# Boulder Commuter Resources & Sustainable Transit Guide

A quick-reference guide for Boulder residents adopting walking, cycling, and public transit for daily commuting.

---

## 🚲 City of Boulder Bicycle Infrastructure

1. **Low-Stress Bike Network:** Boulder features over 300 miles of dedicated bike paths, multi-use trails, and neighborhood greenways with grade-separated underpasses avoiding busy intersections.
2. **Snow & Ice Maintenance:** The City of Boulder prioritizes multi-use paths in its snow removal schedule, often plowing them early in the morning before street lanes are completely cleared.
3. **Vision Zero Boulder:** Boulder is actively upgrading arterial street crossings with Rectangular Rapid Flashing Beacons (RRFBs) and protected intersections (especially on corridors like 19th, 28th, and Broadway).

---

## 🚌 Public Transit Integration: RTD SKIP

- **Route:** Runs along Broadway between North Boulder (Lee Hill / Broadway) and South Boulder (Fairview High / Table Mesa).
- **Frequency:** Every 10–15 minutes on weekdays during peak commute times.
- **Bike Racks:** Every bus has a 3-bike rack on the front bumper. If you experience unexpected weather or a flat tire between Oak Ave and Downtown, the SKIP is your immediate backup.
- **EcoPass:** Many downtown Boulder employers provide free annual RTD EcoPasses through the Downtown Boulder Business Improvement District (BID).

---

## ⚡ Boulder BCycle (E-Bike Share)

- **Fleet:** 100% electric pedal-assist bikes.
- **Stations Near Oak Ave:**
  - North Boulder Park / 8th & Alpine
  - Iris Ave & Broadway
  - 13th & Balsam
- **Stations Near 1800 Broadway:**
  - 14th & Canyon (Boulder Public Library / Municipal Building)
  - 17th & Pearl
  - Downtown Boulder Station (14th & Walnut)
- **Passes:** Free 60-minute trips are often sponsored for students and local community programs.

---

## ❄️ Year-Round Commuting Checklist in Boulder

- **Fall / Spring:** Sudden temperature changes require a windproof shell, light gloves, and polarized sunglasses (high altitude glare).
- **Winter:** 
  - Fenders to guard against slush and magnesium chloride spray.
  - Studded bike tires or wider gravel tires for morning freeze-thaw cycles.
  - Neoprene bar mitts (pogies) and thermal base layers.
  - High-output USB rechargeable front headlight (minimum 400 lumens) and rear flashing red light.
- **Summer:** Hydration pack or water bottle cage, sunscreen, and breathable clothing.
