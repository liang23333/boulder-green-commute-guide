# 🌿 Boulder Green Commute Guide: North Boulder to Downtown

Welcome to the **Boulder Green Commute Guide**! This project provides an in-depth walking, cycling, and multi-modal commute analysis between **North Boulder** and **Downtown Boulder**, offering practical route guidance, environmental impact metrics, and local tips to encourage sustainable transit choices.

---

## 📍 Route Overview

- **Origin:** 45 Oak Avenue, Boulder, CO 80304 (`40.04488° N, -105.27389° W`)
- **Destination:** 1800 Broadway, Boulder, CO 80302 (`40.01633° N, -105.27897° W`)
- **One-Way Distance:** ~4.03 km (2.50 miles)
- **Round-Trip Distance:** ~8.06 km (5.01 miles)
- **Topography:** Gentle net downward slope southbound into the Boulder Creek valley (from ~5,360 ft to ~5,340 ft), offering smooth cycling on the morning commute.

## 🗺️ Turn-by-Turn Route Breakdown (Primary Route via 19th St)

The optimal route balances low-stress neighborhood bikeways, designated bicycle lanes, and safe signalized arterial crossings:

1. **Start on Oak Avenue:** Head east toward 19th Street (*96.6 m / ~317 ft*). Low-traffic residential street.
2. **Turn South onto 19th Street:** Continue south along 19th Street (*2,156.5 m / ~1.34 mi*). 
   - *Features:* Designated bike lanes, sidewalks, and neighborhood greenway characteristics passing North Boulder Park, Alpine Ave, and Balsam Ave.
3. **Transition to 20th Street:** Connect south along 20th Street (*671.7 m / ~0.42 mi*).
   - *Features:* Quiet corridor avoiding heavier Broadway traffic while approaching the downtown perimeter.
4. **Turn West onto Pearl Street:** Proceed west on Pearl Street (*216.2 m / ~709 ft*) toward 18th Street.
5. **Turn South onto 18th Street:** Travel south on 18th Street (*248.8 m / ~816 ft*).
6. **Turn West onto Canyon Boulevard:** Ride west on Canyon Boulevard (*636.6 m / ~0.40 mi*) to the final destination at **1800 Broadway** (intersection with Broadway).

## 🌲 Scenic & Car-Free Alternative Routes

### 1. The Broadway Direct / Multi-Use Path Route (~3.7 km / 2.3 mi)
- Head west on Oak Avenue to Broadway.
- Utilize the multi-use path and bike lanes southbound along Broadway directly to 1800 Broadway.
- *Best for:* Fastest direct cycling; easy backup access to the RTD SKIP bus line.

### 2. The Goose Creek & Boulder Creek Multi-Use Path Variant (~4.4 km / 2.7 mi)
- Head south on 19th Street to the **Goose Creek Path**.
- Connect via off-street paved paths toward the **Boulder Creek Path**, emerging directly into Central Park next to 1800 Broadway.
- *Best for:* Maximum tranquility, zero motor traffic interactions, and scenic creek views.

## 🌱 Environmental & Economic Impact

Choosing active transportation for this daily commute yields substantial benefits:

### 1. Carbon Reduction (EPA Standards)
- **Daily CO₂ Avoided:** ~2.02 kg (4.45 lbs) per day compared to driving an average passenger vehicle.
- **Monthly CO₂ Avoided:** ~40.4 kg (~89 lbs) (20 work days).
- **Annual CO₂ Avoided:** **~485 kg (~1,069 lbs / 0.485 metric tons)** per commuter per year!

### 2. Financial Savings
- **Vehicle Maintenance & Gas:** ~$3.35/day (based on IRS standard rate $0.67/mile) = **~$804/year**.
- **Downtown Boulder Parking Savings:** Metered parking or garage permits at 1800 Broadway/Canyon cost roughly $100–$150/month = **~$1,200–$1,800/year**.
- **Total Estimated Annual Savings:** **$2,000 – $2,600 / year**.

## 🛠️ Boulder Commuter Resources & Tips

- **Bike Parking:** 1800 Broadway and nearby downtown municipal garages (e.g., 14th & Walnut, 11th & Spruce) offer secure bike racks and covered bicycle storage.
- **Weather & Snow Removal:** The City of Boulder clears multi-use paths and primary on-street bike lanes as high-priority routes after snow events.
- **Transit Integration:** RTD's **SKIP** bus line runs North-South along Broadway every 10–15 minutes during peak hours—equipped with front bicycle racks for multi-modal travel.
- **Boulder BCycle:** Boulder's electric bike share system has nearby docks at North Boulder Community Center, 15th & Pearl, and Central Park / Downtown.

For more resources and cold-weather gear advice, see [`boulder-commuter-resources.md`](./boulder-commuter-resources.md).
